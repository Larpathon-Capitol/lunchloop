FoodPredicted demandSafety bufferCook

Predicted demand is how many servings students are expected to take, based on the share of students who took each food over the last 10 school days, adjusted for the day of the week. The safety buffer adds extra so the food rarely runs out (at least 10% for fruit, vegetables and milk). Cook = predicted demand plus the buffer, rounded up. A food with under 3 days of history gets a rough guess from similar foods and is marked.
